`GAME.md` is the in-repo rules and teaching guide for *The Crew: Mission Deep Sea*.

Exit met: independent English summary covering deck, tricks, captain, tasks,
sonar, distress, failure/retry, two-player Tonoja, and common mistakes. It is
not a reproduction of the publisher rulebook or logbook; those remain
authoritative for official mission text. Engine legality in this repo follows
`GAME.md` until a logbook encoding exists.
