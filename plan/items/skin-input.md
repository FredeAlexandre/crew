Pointer-first input: select, then confirm. Keyboard on `desktop` only.

`PRESENTATION.md` §8: tap/click legal card to select, tap again or Play to
commit. Illegal cards muted with why. Hit targets ≥44×44 CSS px. Focus ring
on the UI accent. No hover-only information. Task take is one tap
(`PRESENTATION.md` §18). Keys: arrows, Enter/Space, Esc, S sonar, L last
trick, R reminder, 1–9 jump. Do not steal browser shortcuts.

Acceptance: a mission card can be played on touch and with keyboard; sonar
and distress cannot confirm invalid combinations.
